Executive overview · Linear regression engine

Forecast weekly Walmart store sales with transparent economic intelligence.

This engine applies ordinary least squares linear regression to historical Walmart weekly sales. Dates are parsed into day, month, and year so seasonality, holidays, fuel, CPI, temperature, and unemployment can be scored as additive dollar effects on the next week’s revenue.

Regression methodology

The model estimates weekly sales as y = β₀ + Σ(βᵢ · xᵢ). Coefficients learned from historical store-weeks become dollar weights at prediction time.

Temporal engineering

Calendar dates are split into day, month, and year so the linear model can separate weekday-of-month effects from seasonality and long-run trend.

Decision workflow

Capture a store-week snapshot, generate the forecast, then inspect monthly benchmarks and each variable’s contribution on the analytics dashboard.
